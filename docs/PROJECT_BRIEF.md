# Innes Eye & Visioncare Centre Website

## 1. Project Overview

### Project Title

**Design and Development of a Comprehensive Web-Based Management and Customer Service Platform for Innes Eye & Visioncare Centre, Ghana**

### Project Brief

This project involves the design and development of a fully functional, responsive web-based platform for Innes Eye & Visioncare Centre, an eye-care and optical services provider in Ghana.

The website will serve as a central digital platform where visitors can learn about the centre, explore its eye-care services and optical products, find branch information, request appointments, and contact the business.

In addition to the public-facing website, the system will include a secure administrative dashboard that enables authorised staff to manage appointment requests, customer information, services, optical products, enquiries, and website content. Depending on the centre's confirmed operational requirements, further features may include appointment scheduling, customer accounts, notifications, and operational reports.

The main goal is to strengthen the centre's online presence, make information and services easier to access, improve communication with customers, and support more efficient day-to-day administration across its branches.

> **Project scope note:** This is a proposed system. Confirm the centre's actual workflows and requirements with the business before treating any proposed feature as an existing service or operational process.

## 4. Proposed Website Features and Modules

### 4.1 Public Website

#### Home
- Introduce the centre and its approach to eye care.
- Feature key services and selected optical products.
- Display prominent calls to action, such as **Book Appointment** and **View All Frames**.
- Include branch information and contact details.
- Provide links to helpful eye-health information.

#### About Us
- Present the centre's verified history, mission, vision, and values.
- Introduce practitioners and staff where approved.
- Communicate the centre's experience and approach to customer care.

#### Services
- Display the eye-care services confirmed by the business.
- Provide a clear description of each service.
- Include relevant calls to action, such as requesting an appointment.
- Where services differ by branch, identify the branches that provide them.

Potential service categories to verify include:
- Comprehensive eye examinations
- Vision assessments
- Glaucoma screening
- Contact lens fitting
- Geriatric eye care
- Other specialist services confirmed by the centre

#### Optical Shop / Product Catalogue
- Display optical frames, sunglasses, contact lenses, and lenses where offered.
- Organise products by category.
- Provide product images, descriptions, and relevant details.
- Allow visitors to browse and enquire about product availability.
- Add online purchasing or payment only if the business confirms that it wants and can support e-commerce.

#### Appointment Management
- Provide an online appointment request form.
- Allow customers to select a preferred branch, service, date, and available time where supported.
- Collect only the information needed to process the request.
- Provide confirmation or status updates.
- Allow customers to request rescheduling or cancellation, subject to the centre's policy.
- Give authorised staff the ability to review, approve, reschedule, or cancel requests.

The system should distinguish between an **appointment request** and a **confirmed appointment**. A request should not be described as confirmed until the centre has accepted it.

#### Branch Locator
- List verified branch addresses and contact details.
- Display opening hours for each branch.
- Provide directions or map links.
- Identify the services available at each branch, where applicable.

#### Eye Health / Blog
- Publish educational articles and eye-care tips approved by the business.
- Organise articles by topic.
- Include publication dates and author information where appropriate.
- Avoid presenting general website content as a substitute for professional medical advice.

#### Contact Us
- Display verified telephone numbers, email addresses, and branch details.
- Provide a contact/enquiry form.
- Include links to official social media accounts.
- Explain when and how customers can expect a response, if the business supplies that information.

### 4.2 Customer Features

Depending on the requirements agreed with the business, registered customers may be able to:

- Create an account and sign in securely.
- Submit and view appointment requests.
- View appointment status and history.
- Update basic account details.
- Receive appointment notifications.
- Submit product or service enquiries.

A customer account should not be compulsory for general browsing or for submitting a basic enquiry unless the business has a clear reason to require one.

### 4.3 Administrative Dashboard

The administrative dashboard will be available only to authorised staff.

Proposed functions include:

- **Dashboard overview:** Display useful summaries of appointment requests, enquiries, and catalogue items.
- **Appointment management:** View, approve, reschedule, cancel, and update appointment requests.
- **Customer management:** View and manage customer accounts and relevant contact details.
- **Service management:** Add, edit, publish, or remove service information.
- **Product management:** Add, edit, categorise, and manage optical catalogue items.
- **Branch management:** Maintain branch addresses, contact information, and opening hours.
- **Enquiry management:** Review and respond to customer enquiries.
- **Content management:** Update website text, images, announcements, and eye-health articles.
- **User and role management:** Control staff access according to assigned responsibilities.
- **Reports:** Produce appropriate summaries of appointments, enquiries, and catalogue activity.

The final dashboard should reflect the centre's real administrative workflow rather than include features that staff do not need.

- Use **Deep Navy** to communicate trust and professionalism.
- Use **Teal** for primary action buttons and important interactive elements.
- Use **Soft Sky Blue** for calm backgrounds and selected content sections.
- Use **Warm Sand** sparingly to add warmth and a human touch.
- Use **White** as the main background to keep the interface bright and uncluttered.
- Use **Slate Gray** for readable body text.

Maintain strong text/background contrast. Do not use colour alone to communicate important information, such as appointment status or form errors.

## 6. Recommended Technical and Product Approach

Build the project as a **full-stack web application**, not merely a static business website. This provides both a customer-facing experience and practical tools for the centre's staff.

### Recommended application structure

1. **Public interface:** Pages and features accessible to all visitors.
2. **Customer interface:** Account and appointment features, if customer accounts are required.
3. **Administration interface:** A protected dashboard for authorised staff.
4. **Application/backend layer:** Handles business rules, authentication, appointment workflows, and other operations.
5. **Database layer:** Stores the information required by the application.
6. **Notification layer:** Sends appropriate appointment and enquiry updates, if enabled.

### Recommended implementation principles

- Use a maintainable, modular architecture.
- Separate presentation, application logic, and data access.
- Validate all user input on both the client and server.
- Use secure authentication and role-based authorisation.
- Protect administrative routes and actions.
- Use secure password hashing; never store plain-text passwords.
- Use parameterised database queries or a secure ORM.
- Apply server-side validation even when client-side validation exists.
- Keep secrets and credentials out of source control.
- Use environment variables for configuration.
- Provide useful loading, success, empty, and error states.
- Make the website responsive and test it on common screen sizes.
- Optimise images and page performance.
- Back up important data and plan for recovery.
- Test key customer and administrative workflows before deployment.

The final technology stack should be selected based on the project team's skills, hosting environment, maintenance requirements, and any requirements supplied by the business.

## 7. Privacy, Security, and Healthcare Considerations

Because the project concerns an eye-care provider, privacy and security must be considered from the beginning.

- Collect only the personal information necessary for the stated purpose.
- Restrict patient or medical information to authorised personnel.
- Use role-based access controls and secure sessions.
- Protect data in transit using HTTPS.
- Apply appropriate database access restrictions and backups.
- Record administrative actions where an audit trail is needed.
- Provide clear privacy information and obtain consent where required.
- Establish appropriate retention and deletion procedures.
- Avoid displaying sensitive information in public pages, URLs, logs, or notifications.
- Do not collect or store detailed medical records unless the business has explicitly approved the scope and the system has been designed to handle them securely.

The implementation should be reviewed against applicable Ghanaian data-protection and healthcare requirements. The website is an administrative and customer-service platform; it is not intended to diagnose eye conditions or replace professional clinical judgement.

## 8. Suggested Core User Workflows

### Appointment request workflow

1. A visitor opens the appointment page.
2. The visitor selects a branch and service.
3. The visitor chooses a preferred date and time, if available.
4. The visitor enters the required contact information.
5. The system validates and submits the request.
6. The system displays that the request has been received.
7. An authorised staff member reviews the request.
8. Staff confirm, propose a different time, or decline the request.
9. The customer receives an appropriate status update.

### Product enquiry workflow

1. A visitor opens the optical product catalogue.
2. The visitor browses or filters products.
3. The visitor opens a product details page.
4. The visitor submits an enquiry about the product.
5. The system records the enquiry.
6. Authorised staff review and respond to it.

### Administrative workflow

1. A staff member signs in.
2. The system verifies the staff member's credentials and permissions.
3. The dashboard displays information relevant to that role.
4. The staff member manages appointment requests, enquiries, services, products, or content.
5. The system validates and saves authorised changes.
6. Important actions are recorded where appropriate.
